# Bookmarklet Presentation

Explain why an editor would use a bookmarklet and what visible result to expect. Write for newsroom users rather than developers.

Before producing or revising bookmarklet presentation metadata, read [references/presentation-rules.md](references/presentation-rules.md) completely. It defines:

- the required JSON output schema;
- editorial vocabulary and tone;
- title, summary, effects, usage, and warning limits;
- destructive-action and confidence classification;
- evidence and safety requirements.

Ground every claim in the bookmarklet source or existing metadata. Do not expose implementation details such as DOM traversal, helper names, event dispatch, or transformation pipelines.
